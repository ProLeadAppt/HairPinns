## Blog draft

Title: A kids hair gift they'll actually use (without the guesswork)

Meta description: Jena's simple way to choose a kids hair gift: start with a useful brush, add a little colour if they love it, and skip the things they will not use.

Buying for a kid who already has a cupboard full of stuff? You don't need to add another random box to it.

I like starting with one thing they'll actually use. For some kids, that's a detangling brush for the school-morning hair negotiations. For others, it's a colourful ponytail they can choose themselves. If you know their routine, add a little extra. If you don't, keep it simple.

### Start with their hair, not the prettiest box

Think about what happens on an ordinary day. Do they come home from swimming with tangles? Do they love playing with colourful styles? Or would a practical comb be more useful than another accessory? That tells you more than a gift label ever will.

For detangling, take your time. Start at the ends, work up in small sections and don't try to win an argument with a knot. Hair tends to win that one.

### Let them pick the fun bit

Colour and style are personal, especially when kids know *exactly* which shade they want. Check the available options rather than guessing. If a colour is not listed as available, don't count on it turning up in the box.

### Keep the rest useful

Not sure what suits the child you're buying for? Send me a message and tell me a bit about their hair and what they like. I can point you in the right direction.
